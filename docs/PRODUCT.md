# Product

## Promise

Fitness App turns personal nutrition and weight data into a calm next decision. It shows ranges and trends rather than judging isolated measurements.

## Release 0

- Private onboarding and editable profile.
- Device language detection, ES/EN override, independent units.
- Local migrations, privacy information and full reset.

## Release 1

- Manual calorie and macro/fiber ranges.
- Custom foods, favorites, recent foods, reusable meals and daily logging.
- Daily Balance and Next Best Move from explicit, testable rules.
- Weight history, 7-day moving average, distinct 30-day linear trend and goal progress.

The product provides general wellness guidance, not diagnosis or treatment.
